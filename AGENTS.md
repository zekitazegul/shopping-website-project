# Project instructions

- Build an original marketplace focused on the Netherlands.
- Country filters must refer to the physical location of the item, independently of interface language or seller nationality.
- Keep new and changed user-facing text synchronized in Turkish (tr), English (en), and Dutch (nl).
- Support phone, tablet, and desktop layouts, including portrait and landscape.
- Use free local tooling for now. Do not purchase services or domains, or deploy publicly, without the user's instruction.
- Never commit secrets, credentials, or personal data.
